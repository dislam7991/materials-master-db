**D2, repo hygiene.** `LICENSE` (MIT, 2026 Daniel Islam), `CONTRIBUTING.md`

read SPEC.md §2 and §6 first), and `.gitattributes`.

The line-ending part had the only real decision in it. `* text=auto eol=lf`
plus `*.bat text eol=crlf`: the warnings come from `core.autocrlf` converting
on the Windows machine, and only pinning the working-tree ending overrides
that — declaring what is text would not have quieted anything. The synthetic
CSV is marked `-text` and keeps its CRLF, because `csv.writer` emits CRLF on
every platform; normalizing it would leave every generator run, CI's included,
staring at a whole-file diff, which is the opposite of the DoD.
