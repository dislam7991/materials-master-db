`Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` — the preset identity in the throwaway
container, not a statement about who wrote anything. GitHub only counts a

config. The `Co-Authored-By` trailer stays — author field records whose work

## Judgment calls

- **The §7 rewrite is a full replacement, not an amendment.** The old five
  rules said nothing about open PRs, branch choice, or what to do with a
  broken PR, and the behaviour being fixed lived in the stored scheduler
  prompt rather than in the repo. Patching around that would have left the
  contradiction in place.
- **Cap set at 3 open PRs, then new work stops.** Owner's call. Recorded here
  because "the automation stopped" will otherwise read as a fault later.
- **Log entries push straight to `master`.** Also the owner's call, scoped to
  `docs/runs/**`. It is the one path in this repo that skips review.
- **Past commits left alone.** Re-authoring them means rewriting history
  already merged into `master`; not worth it for a few contribution squares.
